Security at Instinct

An assistant that can act for you needs access to a lot. Here is how that access is protected.

The Vault

In short

Logins Instinct uses for you are stored encrypted.

When Instinct signs in somewhere on your behalf, the credentials live in an encrypted Vault rather than in your chat history or in plain text.

One-time payment cards

In short

Every purchase gets its own single-use card.

Purchases are paid through one-time cards issued via Stripe Link, so a merchant never receives your real card number and a leaked card can’t be reused.

You control access

In short

Instinct reaches only what you connect.

Email, calendar, messages, screen, audio and location are each connected separately. Revoke any of them whenever you like.

Training and your data

In short

Google Workspace data never trains models; you can opt out for everything else.

Instinct does not sell your information. See the privacy policy for details.

Instinct-to-instinct

In short

Assistants talk to each other over an end-to-end encrypted connection.

When your Instinct coordinates with someone else’s, only what the task needs is shared.

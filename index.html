How it works

Connect once. Then just ask.

  1. Connect what matters

    Email, calendar, messages, files and the accounts you use. You decide what Instinct can reach.

  2. It learns your context

    Instinct’s core model understands what you’re working on and what’s important to you — and it remembers.

  3. It acts, and checks in

    It books, buys, follows up and calls, and comes back to you when a decision is yours to make.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Instinct?

Instinct is a personal assistant that understands what you’re working on and what’s important to you. It connects to your applications and devices — email, messaging, screen, audio, location and more — and takes action on your behalf.

How do I talk to Instinct?

The same way you talk to a person: text it or call it. There is no new interface to learn. Instinct works over iMessage and WhatsApp, and there is a Mac app.

What can Instinct do for me?

Everyday errands and follow-through: picking up threads you dropped, arranging a ride to the airport, booking travel and appointments, ordering what you need, and reaching out to you first when something needs your attention.

How does Instinct keep my accounts and payments safe?

Logins live in an encrypted Vault, and purchases use one-time payment cards, so your real card number is never handed to a merchant. You choose which apps and devices Instinct can access.

Does Instinct use my data to train AI?

Data from Google Workspace is used only to provide the service and is never used to train models. For other data, you can opt out of AI training. See the privacy policy.

Can Instinct work with other people’s assistants?

Yes. With instinct-to-instinct, your assistant can coordinate directly with someone else’s over an end-to-end encrypted connection — to find a time, split a booking or hand off a task.

How do I get access?

Instinct is currently invite-only for people 18 and over. Text Instinct to get started and join the list.
